## Authority and minimal market-case path

[TDD §11.1, lines 1576–1580](../../../03_Tank_V0_Technical_Design.md#111-replay-harness) requires authentic sources/timestamps, market references, registry/config versions, cutoff isolation, pre-run sealed outcomes, no internet and identity masking. [§11.3, lines 1628–1633](../../../03_Tank_V0_Technical_Design.md#113-fixtures-f-01f-27) defines F-11 as a “Market Truth validation case” and F-12–F-16 as market windows. Line 1646 says those windows were discovered by scanning `openInterestHist` and klines and their packets use archive/REST sources. It does **not** prescribe a separate economic catalyst or MAJOR event for these cases.

The universal NEW_ITEM restriction is in `fixture_reference.py:154–165` and `replay.py:1531`, not those market-case definitions. Even the independent readiness checker already exempts MARKET_CASES from `ingest.source_items` at `tests/fixture_acceptance.py:326–328`. The authentic source evidence for these cases is the original market response/archive and its receipt; inventing a press release would weaken provenance.

The minimal test-only path reuses existing `prepare_masked_packet`, `replay_bundle` and `ReplayMarketReader`: assemble the retained raw-market references and original call-window/cutoff, keep the map and unchanged TDD assertions outside inputs, seal before execution, compute production Truth/Decision in a fresh empty repository, freeze output, then evaluate every literal clause. A later fresh `tank-replay` session can read only that isolated data and produce market wording without `record_event`, `set_assessment` or publication. This is a validation lane, not a new production trigger or CLI mode. Code/gate rewrites are not required merely to run a truthful negative market case. Native wording and its receipts must be reported separately; a formula run is not native acceptance or latency evidence.

No confirmed new narrow product defect was established by this read-only audit.
